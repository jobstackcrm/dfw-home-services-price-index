# DFW Home Services Price Index — Fall 2026

Planning price ranges for common residential electrical, plumbing and HVAC work in the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, compiled by [JobStack](https://my.jobstackcrm.com/research/dfw-home-services-price-index).

> These are planning ranges — not quotes, bids or guarantees. Compare equivalent written line-item scopes, not just totals.

## Key numbers (Fall 2026)

| Item | Typical DFW range | Planning midpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electrical panel upgrade to 200A | $1,250–$2,800 | ≈ $1,650 |
| Level 2 EV charger installation | $700–$1,800 | ≈ $1,250 |
| Tank water-heater replacement (40–50 gal) | $1,050–$2,000 | ≈ $1,490 |
| AC repair | $150–$1,150 | ≈ $350 |
| Full AC + furnace system replacement | $8,300–$16,000 | ≈ $10,000–$11,000 |

Full table (16 line items across electrical, plumbing and HVAC) is in [`dfw-home-services-price-index-fall-2026.csv`](dfw-home-services-price-index-fall-2026.csv).

## Methodology

Fall 2026 edition, compiled November 2026. JobStack synthesized published Dallas–Fort Worth pricing from local contractor pricing pages and localized cost-guide data (including Angi Dallas cost data, HomeGuide and regional cost indexes), cross-checked across at least two sources per line item. Future editions may add anonymized first-party quote ranges from services booked through JobStack.

Full methodology and sources: https://my.jobstackcrm.com/research/dfw-home-services-price-index
